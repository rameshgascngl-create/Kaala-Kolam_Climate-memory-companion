## Full description — English
Kaala Kolam (காலக்கோலம்) is a bilingual English–Tamil learning app that connects local climate memory with scientific ideas about weather, climate, risk and adaptation.

Explore structured Learn topics with step-by-step animated explanations; record an Elders interview about environmental change; exchange compact Class codes without an account or server; examine adaptation choices in the Council activity; and test prediction confidence in the Predict section. Safety learning covers hazards including heat, floods and cyclones.

The app works fully offline, has no advertising or analytics, requests no internet access and collects no data. Read-aloud uses the device's speech facilities, with an Android text-to-speech fallback when WebView speech voices are unavailable.

Department of Zoology, GASC, Nagercoil / Created by R. Ramesh.

Educational use only. This is not an official warning service. Follow IMD and State Disaster Management Authority advice for current warnings, and verify emergency numbers for your district.
