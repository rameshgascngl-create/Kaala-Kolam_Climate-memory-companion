Kaala Kolam (காலக்கோலம்) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Kaala Kolam is an educational application from the Department of Zoology, GASC, Nagercoil, created by R. Ramesh.

Data collection and sharing

Kaala Kolam collects no personal data and shares no data with third parties. It has no account system, advertising, analytics or internet permission.

Information stored on the device

Interviews, predictions, council choices, learning progress and preferences are stored only in the app's private local storage on the user's device. The developer does not receive this information. Uninstalling the app removes the app's private local data. Class codes are copied and pasted by the user and are not transmitted by the app.

Read-aloud

Read-aloud uses speech facilities installed on the device. If WebView speech voices are unavailable, the app can use Android TextToSpeech on the device. The application itself does not send read-aloud text to the developer.

Children and learners

The app is educational and may be used by learners of different ages. It does not collect personal data from users.

Safety information

The app is for educational use and is not an official warning service. For current warnings and emergency guidance, follow the India Meteorological Department (IMD) and the relevant State Disaster Management Authority. Verify emergency numbers for your district.
