Kaala Kolam is an educational Android application created by R. Ramesh, Department of Zoology, GASC, Nagercoil. It works fully offline.

Data

The app collects no personal data, shares no data, uses no analytics or advertising, and requests no Android permissions. Interviews, class codes, predictions, settings and progress are stored locally on the device in WebView localStorage and are not transmitted by the app.

Network access

The Android app does not request the INTERNET permission. Links deliberately opened from the native About menu are handed to a separate system browser or mail application.

Educational disclaimer

This app is for educational use and is not an official warning service. Follow the India Meteorological Department and the relevant State Disaster Management Authority for official warnings. Emergency numbers should be verified for your district.
